Prohibited Items Policy

Last updated:

SkoolSwap is a marketplace designed for school communities. To protect our users and comply with South African law, certain items may not be listed, sold, exchanged, or promoted on the Platform.

If a listing violates this policy, SkoolSwap may remove it without notice and may suspend or permanently terminate the seller's account.

1. Purpose

This Prohibited Items Policy helps ensure that SkoolSwap remains a safe, trustworthy, and legally compliant marketplace for parents, guardians, learners, educators, and schools.

Users are responsible for ensuring that every item they list complies with this policy and all applicable laws.

2. Items Allowed on SkoolSwap

Examples of items that may be listed include:

  • School uniforms
  • Sports uniforms
  • School shoes
  • Blazers
  • Jerseys
  • Hats and caps
  • School bags
  • Lunch bags
  • Stationery
  • Textbooks
  • Study guides
  • Calculators
  • Musical instruments
  • Sports equipment
  • School accessories
  • Arts and crafts supplies
  • Approved educational technology (such as tablets or laptops intended for learning)

All items must comply with these Terms and be accurately described.

3. Illegal Items

The following items are strictly prohibited:

  • Stolen property
  • Counterfeit goods
  • Forged certificates or documents
  • Fake school uniforms intended to mislead buyers
  • Illegal software
  • Pirated media
  • Any item whose sale is prohibited under South African law

4. Weapons and Dangerous Items

You may not list:

  • Firearms
  • Ammunition
  • Explosives
  • Fireworks
  • Knives intended as weapons
  • Tasers
  • Pepper spray
  • Crossbows
  • BB guns or air rifles where prohibited
  • Any dangerous weapon or accessory

5. Alcohol, Tobacco and Drugs

Listings may not include:

  • Alcohol
  • Cigarettes
  • Cigars
  • Vapes or e-cigarettes
  • Tobacco products
  • Nicotine products
  • Illegal drugs
  • Controlled substances
  • Drug paraphernalia
  • Prescription medication

6. Medical and Health Products

The following are prohibited:

  • Prescription medicines
  • Medical devices requiring professional authorization
  • Used syringes or needles
  • Blood testing kits
  • Biological samples
  • Hazardous medical waste

7. Adult Content

You may not list:

  • Pornographic material
  • Sexually explicit products
  • Adult toys
  • Escort or adult services
  • Content exploiting or endangering children

Any content involving child exploitation will be reported to the appropriate authorities.

8. Animals and Plants

Listings may not include:

  • Live animals
  • Animal parts prohibited by law
  • Protected wildlife
  • Endangered species
  • Illegal plants or seeds

9. Food and Beverages

Unless specifically approved by SkoolSwap, users may not sell:

  • Homemade food
  • Perishable food
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Nutritional supplements making medical claims
  • Expired food products

10. Digital Products

The following may not be listed without written approval from SkoolSwap:

  • Gift cards
  • Digital accounts
  • Streaming subscriptions
  • Software licences
  • Cryptocurrency
  • NFTs
  • Download codes

11. Financial Products

Listings may not include:

  • Loans
  • Insurance products
  • Investment opportunities
  • Shares or securities
  • Credit services
  • Money transfers

12. Hazardous Materials

The following are prohibited:

  • Chemicals
  • Pesticides
  • Explosives
  • Toxic substances
  • Flammable liquids
  • Radioactive materials
  • Corrosive products

13. Stolen or Counterfeit Goods

You must not sell items that:

  • Have been stolen.
  • Are counterfeit.
  • Infringe trademarks or copyrights.
  • Misrepresent a brand or manufacturer.

Where ownership is disputed, SkoolSwap may remove the listing while the matter is investigated.

14. Recalled or Unsafe Products

Items subject to a manufacturer recall or known to present a safety risk may not be listed.

Sellers should ensure that products offered for sale are safe and suitable for their intended purpose.

15. Inappropriate Listings

Listings may also be removed if they:

  • Promote hate or discrimination.
  • Encourage violence.
  • Contain offensive language.
  • Include misleading photographs.
  • Use false pricing.
  • Are duplicates created to manipulate search results.
  • Contain spam or unrelated advertising.

16. Enforcement

If a listing violates this policy, SkoolSwap may:

  • Remove the listing.
  • Issue a warning.
  • Restrict marketplace privileges.
  • Suspend the account.
  • Permanently terminate the account.
  • Cancel related transactions.
  • Delay or withhold escrow payouts while investigating.
  • Report unlawful activity to the relevant authorities where required by law.

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate permanent suspension without prior warning.

17. Reporting Prohibited Items

If you believe a listing violates this policy, you can report it through the Platform.

When submitting a report, please include:

  • The listing.
  • The reason for your report.
  • Any supporting information or evidence.

SkoolSwap will review reports as quickly as reasonably possible. Submitting false or malicious reports may result in action against your account.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Prohibited Items Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law, marketplace practices, or Platform features.

The latest version will always be available on https://skoolswap.co.za.

19. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to report a prohibited item, please contact:

Support Email: admin@skoolswap.co.za

Website: skoolswap.co.za

Thank you for helping us keep SkoolSwap a safe and trusted marketplace for school communities.
